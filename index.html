About

ForgeVerse connects NFTs, gaming, and a resource-driven economy. Each NFT has a role: Traders earn $DOLLAR, Farmers grow $FOOD, and Workers mine $METAL. Use resources to feed, forge, and fuse your NFTs.

ForgeVerse Whitepaper

  1. Vision – Retro pixel economy where NFTs generate tradable resources on Solana.
  2. Core Loop – Mint → Stake → Earn → Upgrade → Fuse → Play → Repeat.
  3. NFT Roles – 1000 Builders: 10% Traders, 30% Farmers, 60% Workers.
  4. Resources – $DOLLAR (trading power), $FOOD (feeding/boosts), $METAL (tools/crafting).
  5. Sinks – Each token feeds its own sink to avoid runaway inflation.
  6. Fusion Lab – Burn 2 NFTs + resources → stronger NFT, lowering supply over time.
  7. Game – Pac-Forge minigame adds skill-based boosts.
  8. Roadmap – Genesis → Economy → Fusion → Game expansion → Social Forge.
