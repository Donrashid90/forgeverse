About

ForgeVerse connects NFTs, gaming, and a resource-driven economy. Each NFT has a role: Traders earn $DOLLAR, Farmers grow $FOOD, and Workers mine $METAL. Use resources to feed, forge, and fuse your NFTs.

ForgeVerse Docs

1. Vision

ForgeVerse is a retro pixel economy on Solana where NFTs generate tradable resources. Every Builder belongs to one role: Traders earn $DOLLAR, Farmers grow $FOOD, Workers mine $METAL.

2. Core Loop

  • Mint / buy Builders (NFTs)
  • Stake them to generate resources
  • Spend resources to upgrade production
  • Fuse Builders for stronger units
  • Play the mini-game for boosts

3. NFTs & Roles

Total supply: 1000 Builders. 10% Traders, 30% Farmers, 60% Workers. Every role has different yields and upgrade paths.

4. Resources

  • $DOLLAR – trading power, premium upgrades, marketplace utility
  • $FOOD – feeding NFTs to increase efficiency & sustain growth
  • $METAL – forging tools and crafting upgrades

5. Upgrades & Sinks

Resources are not inflationary forever. Each token fuels its own sink: FOOD → feeding, METAL → forging tools, DOLLAR → trading rigs and premium features.

6. Fusion Lab

Two Builders + resources can be fused into one stronger Builder. Fusion burns NFTs, reducing supply over time and stabilizing rewards.

7. Game & Social

Pac-Forge mini-game rewards active players with temporary boosts. Chat & guilds create community retention and long-term engagement.

8. Roadmap

Progressive rollout: Genesis → Economy → Fusion → Game Expansion → Social Forge → DAO-lite.
