About

ForgeVerse is a retro pixel economy where NFTs have jobs. Traders earn $DOLLAR, Farmers produce $FOOD, and Workers mine $METAL.

ForgeVerse Whitepaper (Short)

Vision: A sustainable on-chain pixel economy.

Core Loop: Mint → Stake → Earn → Upgrade → Fuse → Repeat.

  • $DOLLAR = trading power + premium upgrades
  • $FOOD = feeding NFTs to boost efficiency
  • $METAL = forging tools & crafting upgrades

Fusion: Burn 2 NFTs + resources → 1 stronger Builder.
